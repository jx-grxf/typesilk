english.txt
  From "FrequencyWords" by Hermit Dave, built from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus.
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
  Names were removed by checking their casing against the Wiktionary frequency list
  "English/Wikipedia (2016)", which is based on the Leipzig Corpora Collection.

german.txt
  From the Wiktionary frequency list "German/Mixed web 3M", based on the Leipzig
  Corpora Collection, cross-checked against the German "FrequencyWords" list.
  https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Frequency_lists/German/Mixed_web_3M

Changes: only plain words were kept, offensive words and names were removed, order is by
frequency. scripts/build-wordlists.mjs rebuilds both files.
