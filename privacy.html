Privacy

Typing on typesilk needs no account. Without one, your runs, settings and profile stay in your browser and never reach our database. If you sign in, your profile stats are also stored with your account so they show up on every device you sign in on.

Who is responsible

Johannes Grof, Mettersdorf am Saßbach, Austria, contact@johannesgrof.me.

If you sign in

Signing in with GitHub or Google is optional. Without it you miss the leaderboard and your stats stay on one device. We store:

  • the account id your provider gives us, which tells us it's you again
  • the username and display name you choose, both public on the leaderboard
  • your ranked daily runs: speed, accuracy, duration, date and a fingerprint of the keystrokes that catches copied runs
  • your profile stats from each browser you sign in on: level points, runs, words, best speed per setup, recent speeds, per-letter error and speed counts, typing per day, dailies, badges and streak. The first sign-in in a browser adds the stats it already has.
  • a hash of your session token

We don't ask for your email address. We don't store your real name or photo from Google, or your picture from GitHub. If your GitHub profile has a public name, we suggest it as your display name and you can change it before anything is public. Your keystrokes are sent to check a run and then discarded.

We process this to provide the leaderboard and the stats sync you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Runs that look automated are hidden from the board automatically. If that happens to you by mistake, write to us and a person will look at it.

How long we keep it

Account data, ranked runs and synced stats stay until you delete your account. The reset in settings clears the synced stats on every device. Sessions end after 30 days or when you sign out. Deleted data can remain in database backups for up to 30 days.

Cookies and browser storage

Signing in sets two cookies that the sign-in can't work without: one for up to 10 minutes during sign-in, and one for up to 30 days that keeps you signed in. Your browser also keeps your settings and stats in local storage. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Fonts are served from this site.

Who processes the data

The site and database run on Cloudflare, which acts as our processor under its data processing addendum. It handles your IP address and request data to deliver the site, limit abuse and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare can process data outside the EU, including in the US, where it's certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

During sign-in, GitHub or Google handles your login as its own controller under GitHub's or Google's privacy policy. We don't share your data with anyone else or sell it.

Deleting your data

Your profile page has a delete account button. It removes your account, ranked runs and synced stats straight away. Each browser keeps the runs typed in it: clear this site's data in your browser or use the reset in settings.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, and object to processing. Write to the address above. You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority (dsb.gv.at).
